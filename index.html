Gutter guards in Canton, North Canton and Akron

Stark County has a lot of mature maple, oak and pine, which means shingle grit, helicopter seeds and needles that clog the cheap screen guards from the hardware store within a season. We install stainless micro-mesh on your existing 5-inch or 6-inch gutters, fastened to the gutter lip and roof edge, not screwed through your shingles. That matters on the two-story colonials in North Canton and the older ranches around Perry and Jackson Township where fascia has seen 30 winters. Every install includes a cleaning, a reseal of leaky corners, and photos before and after. We work from Massillon east to Alliance and north through Green, Uniontown and Akron.

Questions people ask us

How much do gutter guards cost in Canton?

Most homes run $12 to $20 per linear foot installed. A typical 150 to 200 foot house lands between $2,200 and $3,500. Two-story homes and steep roofs are on the higher end. We text you a range before anyone comes out.

Do I need to be home for the quote?

No. Send the address and we measure from satellite. The on-site visit is 20 minutes and only to confirm footage and check the fascia. You can be there or not.

Do you clean the gutters before installing guards?

Yes, every time. We clean, flush the downspouts, reseal leaky corners and fix pitch problems before guards go on. Guards on a clogged or sagging gutter just hide the problem.

What's the warranty?

The guard itself carries the manufacturer's 50-year warranty against defects. Our installation is covered by a 5-year workmanship warranty. If it leaks or lifts, we come back.

How is this different from LeafFilter?

Same category of stainless micro-mesh product, without the in-home presentation or the sign-tonight discount. One price, good for a year, usually $1,500 to $3,000 less on the same house. Send us their quote and we'll tell you straight.

Do you serve Akron and Hudson?

Yes. We cover Stark County and the south half of Summit County, including Akron, Green, Hudson, Stow and Cuyahoga Falls. Medina and Wooster on request.
